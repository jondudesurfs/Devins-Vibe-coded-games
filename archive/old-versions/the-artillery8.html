The Artillery

Defend your fortress! Here is how:
• Aim the cannon by moving your mouse. It auto-fires every couple of seconds and BOOMS a whole group.
• Swordsmen charge out of your fort on their own to fight the enemy.
• Click the glowing side circles to send your 2 flank fighters there. They shoot enemies marching past.
• Every 100 points your fort levels up: the cannon fires faster, archers march out to shoot from long range, and the fort gains health, but the enemy army gets stronger too!
• Every enemy you defeat drops coins (🪙 1 for red, 3 for purple, 10 for a boss). After each wave the Upgrade Shop opens, spend coins to power up your cannon, swordsmen, and archers, buy extra flank fighters, or recruit whole batches of troops at once! Upgrades cost more each time, and the enemy grows bigger and faster every wave.
• Level 3: an armored Knight roams the whole battlefield hunting enemies, and a second cannon appears that auto-fires every 3 seconds.
• The world changes every 4 levels! Level 4 is the Wild West (cowboys join you and outlaws attack), Level 8 is the High Seas (everyone fights from rowboats, friendly pirates roam and shoot, and enemy pirates row in from the sides), Level 12 returns to the Castle, and it keeps rotating.
• Every level-up sends a mini-boss: lots of health, hits hard. Gang up on it!
• Don't let the red army reach your fort!
Made for you. Good luck, Commander.
