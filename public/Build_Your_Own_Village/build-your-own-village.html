Build Your Own Village from Scratch

  • You start with a Town Hall, a Farm, and a Guard Stand.
  • Defeat enemies to get gold. Spend it on walls, spikes, and buildings.
  • Farms make food. Use food to hire archers and swordsmen.
  • Archers stand on top of buildings. Swordsmen live at Guard Stands.
  • Survive 20 waves of goblins, knights, monsters, and robots to conquer the world!
